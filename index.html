Before taking medicine

Four simple safety checks

These general checks can help reduce avoidable medication mistakes.

1
Confirm the medicine

Check the name, strength, and expiry date on the package.

2
Follow professional advice

Use the dose and schedule advised by your clinician or pharmacist.

3
Check allergies & interactions

Tell your healthcare professional about allergies and other medicines you use.

4
Watch for serious reactions

Seek urgent medical care for severe allergic reactions, breathing difficulty, or other alarming symptoms.
